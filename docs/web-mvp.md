## 4–8. Architecture, parity, scene and input

The [architecture decision](web-architecture.md) records the native audit and
choice before implementation. TypeScript + Vite produces a static website;
Three.js draws original procedural geometry. MediaPipe Tasks Vision executes
locally in a dedicated worker. There is no server application, React, database,
account, analytics, cloud telemetry or stored calibration. The visitor only opens
the website and grants camera permission; developer tools are not visitor prerequisites.

Native Python/Blender remains intact and independently usable. JSON fixtures are
generated by calling native Python functions, not by reproducing their outputs
manually. Tests compare native poses, geometry, four collision sweeps, planes,
raw landmarks, calibration, filtered commands and 50 task/telemetry transitions
with numerical tolerances (normally 1e-9; pivot tolerance 1e-6 m).

The native Z-up metre coordinate system, camera, board, six numbered source rings,
six reflected targets, handoff region and instrument collision geometry are
retained. All 13 critical positions are inverse-reachable by both instruments
with 10% joint margin and inside the camera frame. A complete constrained route
checks board/workspace/pair clearance every tick. Fixed 4:3 camera optics are
letterboxed on resize, preserving containment geometry. Cylindrical rendered
shafts/jaws follow collision segment endpoints; the capsule proxies conservatively
round their ends. This is rigid procedural trainer geometry, not tissue physics.

Camera permission, denial, absence, busy-device and secure-context cases have
actionable messages. Preview and detector input are mirrored consistently. Two
physical-hand identities use the native label correction; palm center, apparent
scale, orientation and calibrated thumb/index pinch drive independent commands.
Freshness is limited to 0.5 s. Loss holds the affected tool and preserves calibration;
the other valid tool can continue. Reacquisition is rate-limited. Physical identity,
comfort and camera-specific depth/roll quality must still be checked manually.

Guided flow: CAMERA SETUP → show both hands → HOME/neutral → OPEN → PINCH →
CALIBRATION COMPLETE → five uninterrupted ready seconds near HOME → LIVE.
Each calibration hold uses 0.75 s preparation plus 3 s stillness and at least
30 paired samples, captured atomically using medians. Motion, loss, duplicate
identity, poor framing or insufficient OPEN/CLOSED separation reject/reset holds.
HOME references are personal and remain visible during LIVE; they are not motion
bounds and returning there does not recalibrate. Recalibration requires a deliberate
paused, empty-handed action. Hidden tabs pause the session.

## 9–12. Mechanics, constraints, task and metrics

Yaw ±35°, pitch ±25°, insertion 0.12–0.28 m, roll ±180°, jaws 0–1. Tool movement
uses native bounded rates and a maximum 0.05 s controller step. Both pivots remain
at (±0.085, 0, 0.12) m. Maximum reconstructed pivot error over **486 poses** was
**1.3877787807814457e-17 m**, tolerance **1e-6 m**. Actual Three rendered shaft
endpoints in the browser trial measured **2.8609792490763984e-17 m** maximum error.
Numerical pivot accuracy does not establish clinical realism.

Swept capsule constraints enforce tool/tool, board and workspace/camera boundaries,
including support clearance of held rings. Contacts can be retreated from. Repeated
commands into a blocked boundary add no fake path. No unconstrained teleport is
used to complete the controller-driven trial.

Exclusive ownership, edge-triggered pinch grasp/release, grasp offsets, armed
receiver handoff, occupied-target protection, correct/incorrect placement, drop
settling, six-ring completion and reset are implemented. The end-to-end trial
performs six bimanual handoffs and six correct placements; separate native fixture
transitions cover wrong placement and drop. Node used 452 controller ticks; Chrome
used 450 because of floating-point threshold differences. Outcomes and constraints
agree; controller tick counts are not a parity assertion.

Results show active completion time, objects/total, grasps, drops, handoffs,
incorrect/successful placements, left/right/total paths. Time starts at first grasp,
excludes pauses and includes live tracking-loss time as native Phase 4 does. Paths
observe accepted constrained shaft references with a 0.5 mm accumulation threshold
and pause reanchoring. Histories are bounded, results immutable, and Retry resets
task, counters, paths, contacts and LIVE gate while preserving usable calibration.
There is no proficiency or competence score, export/upload or research collection.

3. Show both hands fully in frame under even light. Check physical Left/Right

4. Hold a comfortable HOME/neutral pose until captured automatically. Deliberately
   move once during a hold to confirm its countdown resets.
5. Follow OPEN, holding thumb/index apart near the personal HOME references.
6. Follow PINCH, closing thumb/index near HOME. Confirm insufficient separation is
   rejected, then provide distinct OPEN/PINCH calibration if asked.
7. Confirm CALIBRATION COMPLETE and the automatic five-second ready countdown.
   Remove one hand during it: countdown must cancel/restart when both return.
8. Enter LIVE without keyboard setup shortcuts. Move both instruments independently;
   verify fulcrum reversal and smooth bounded yaw/pitch.
9. Move each hand toward/away from the camera to test insertion; twist the palm to
   test roll; open/pinch to test jaws without excessive steering disturbance.
10. Approach a ring with open jaws, pinch to grasp, lift, open to release. Confirm
    no teleport on pickup. Release away from a target to verify a drop.
11. Grasp again, bring both contacts together, close the receiver while the donor
    holds, then open the donor. Confirm exclusive ownership and a handoff.
12. Try a mismatched target, then correct it. Verify target numbering and correct
    placement; complete all six matching targets through grasp/handoff/release.
13. During play test tool/tool and board contact and retreat. Test viewport/workspace
    edges and depth limits; held rings must remain within the working volume.
14. Remove one hand during LIVE: its tool must hold; calibration/HOME must survive.
    Return it and confirm bounded recovery. HOME must not constrain LIVE movement.
15. Pause/resume and briefly hide the tab: motion must hold; paused time/path must
    not be counted. Resume with both hands visible.
16. Confirm COMPLETE and all ten result metrics (time, objects, grasps, drops,
    handoffs, incorrect/successful placements, left/right/total path), without any
    surgical competence claim.
17. Click Retry: verify six source rings, zero counters/path, no owners/results or
    contact carryover, retained HOME/calibration and a fresh ready countdown.
18. Stop camera: the browser camera indicator should turn off. Reload: no session
    state should persist. Separately deny camera permission and verify a safe,
    actionable retry state. Confirm usability and frame rate on your actual hardware.
